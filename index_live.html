Trust & Safety

Built around trust.

Peer-to-peer rentals work only when both sides trust the platform. SkipIt is being built with multiple layers of trust mechanisms designed for the Indian rental market.

Identity & KYC Verification

Users verify their identity before accessing the marketplace. KYC helps establish accountability and builds trust between renters and owners.

User Profiles & Reviews

Transparent profiles with ratings and reviews from past rentals. A track record that helps both renters and owners make informed decisions.

Listing Moderation

All listings go through automated checks before going live. Our system screens for prohibited items, suspicious content, and policy violations.

Fraud & Spam Detection

Automated systems monitor for suspicious activity, duplicate listings, abnormal pricing, and fraudulent behaviour across the platform.

How SkipIt earns

A marketplace designed to scale.

SkipIt generates revenue primarily through a commission on successful rental transactions. The model is straightforward and transparent.

Platform Revenue
12 %

Commission per transaction

SkipIt earns a 12% commission on every successful rental transaction completed through the platform. This is the primary revenue mechanism.

Charged on rental value only
Optional protection plan fees
No hidden platform charges
Security Mechanism

Rental deposits — not platform revenue

Rental deposits may be required for certain items, categories, or risk levels. It is important to understand the distinction:

Deposits are held security
They are returned after a successful rental is completed. They are not earned by SkipIt.
Commission is platform revenue
Only the 12% commission on the rental value is retained by SkipIt as earnings.

Note on logistics: SkipIt does not provide any logistics or delivery service. Pickup and return arrangements are made directly between the renter and owner according to the rental agreement.

Platform technology

More than listings. A smarter marketplace.

Automated Listing Checks

Listings are reviewed against content policies before going live on the marketplace.

Duplicate Detection

Suspicious keywords and patterns are flagged before listing publication.

Fraud Indicators

Human Moderation
